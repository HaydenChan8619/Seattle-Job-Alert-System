# Design System & Aesthetics: Seattle Career Monitor (Soft Pastel Brutalism)

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World: "Soft Pastel Brutalism"

A refined, tactile, soft-brutalist interface that tones down the harshness of traditional brutalism. It combines a restrained 2-accent pastel palette (Soft Mint `#dcfce7`, Soft Butter `#fef9c3`), dark slate 2px borders (`#1e293b`), subtle offset shadows (`3px 3px 0px 0px #1e293b`), and balanced, readable typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans` + `Space Mono`).

---

## Palette & Surface Roles

- **Canvas Background**: Warm cream (`#faf8f5`).
- **Card Surfaces**: Crisp pure white (`#ffffff`) with 2px dark slate borders (`#1e293b`) and subtle 3px offset shadows (`shadow-[3px_3px_0px_0px_#1e293b]`).
- **Primary Accent**: Soft Pastel Mint (`bg-[#dcfce7]` / `#bbf7d0`) for active buttons, submission counts, and primary actions.
- **Secondary Accent**: Soft Pastel Butter (`bg-[#fef9c3]`) for new drop notifications and action badges.
- **Muted Accent**: Soft Slate (`bg-[#e2e8f0]`) for secondary indicators.
- **Borders & Typography**: Refined Dark Slate (`#1e293b`) instead of stark pitch black.

---

## Typography & Tactile Controls

- **Display Titles**: Balanced `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`font-bold text-lg text-[#1e293b]`).
- **Data & Timestamps**: Clean `Space Mono` (`font-bold text-xs/3xl`).
- **Action Buttons**: Soft-brutal buttons with `2px` slate borders, `2px` offset shadows, and subtle tactile hover/active shifts (`translate(-1px, -1px)` and `translate(1px, 1px)`).
